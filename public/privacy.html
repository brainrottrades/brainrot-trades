Brainrot Trades is a fan-made price guide and meeting place for Fortnite Steal the Brainrot (the Fortnite Creative map, island code 3225-0366-8885) — Eternals only. No Secrets and no base brainrots can be listed or traded here. This site is not the Roblox game Steal a Brainrot, and it is not affiliated with Epic Games, Fortnite, SpyderSammy, or the Roblox game. All trademarks belong to their owners.

Brainrot pictures on this site are fan images used to identify the characters in the price guide. If you own an image shown here and want it removed, contact pgevil22@gmail.com and it will be taken down.

What we collect

  • Your account: username, email address, and a password (stored hashed — never in plain text).
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address and Google account ID so we can verify it is you. We do not get your Google password.
  • Payments: if you subscribe, PayPal gives us your subscription ID and status so we can turn on your access. We never see your card or bank details.
  • What you post: chat messages, trade posts, sale listings, comments, and private messages you send on the site.

How we use it

To run the site, verify accounts, turn on subscriptions, and show your posts to other members. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Who else sees it

  • PayPal processes subscription payments (their privacy policy applies to the payment itself).
  • Google verifies your identity when you sign in with Google.
  • Other members see your username and anything you post publicly on the site. Private messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation, unless a message is reported — then the owner can see that one message.

Cookies

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in. It contains no personal information beyond your login session.

Deleting your account

Ask the owner (pgevil22@gmail.com) and your account and posts will be deleted. See data deletion instructions.
